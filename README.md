# DigitalMetal CDT Summer Hackathon 2026

This repository presents our group project, developed in collaboration with the Manufacturing Technology Centre (MTC), exploring a digital twin prototype for quality monitoring in laser powder bed fusion (LPBF). We investigated how melt pool monitoring, optical thermography, and X-ray computed tomography could support defect analysis and part-quality prediction. The repository includes our project abstract, a video demonstration of the Streamlit dashboard, and each team member’s code. The work is an exploratory prototype requiring further validation before live manufacturing use. Original project data are confidential and are not included.

# Project Abstract

Approximately 40% of the cost incurred during the additive manufacturing (AM) of parts
is from post processing and quality assurance through non-destructive testing (NDT).
Correlating numerous amounts of in-process monitoring data with product quality
before post-processing can identify the window for significant financial savings before
NDT. Aluminium CP1 powder is used for laser power bed fusion (LPBF) because the
aluminium has a narrow processing window, and a higher thermal conductivity whilst
having a high reflectivity at near infra-red and the CP1 encourages a more heterogeneous
nucleation, a finer grain structure and crack resilience. Melt pool measurements, optical
thermography and XCT scans were utilised to compare outcomes of three different
geometries of parts (15mm, 10mm and 5mm) to integrate into a unified digital twin to aid
in identifying in-situ process signatures that could support real-time prediction of
porosity or part failure. However, the current analysis did not achieve true in-situ
prediction, but MPM data were analysed post-build with the aim of later correlating with
XCT-derived porosity and part status to determine the relationship between melt pool
signatures and defect formation. Furthermore, this work provides a foundation in future
in-situ defect prediction by developing and validating models that relate process
monitoring data to defect detection and quality assurance.
